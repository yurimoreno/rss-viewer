### US-002: Populate sidebar from imported feeds
Status: in_progress
Depends on: US-001

Description:
As a user, I want imported OPML feeds to appear in the sidebar under their folders.

Acceptance Criteria:
- [ ] Parse OPML folder names into categories
- [ ] Render feeds in the sidebar grouped by category
- [ ] Feeds without a folder appear under "Uncategorized"
- [ ] Selecting a feed loads it in the reader
