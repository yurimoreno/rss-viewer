### US-004: Remember recent feeds
Status: in_progress
Depends on: US-003

Description:
As a user, I want quick access to recent feeds so I can revisit them easily.

Acceptance Criteria:
- [ ] Store the last 5 successfully loaded feed URLs in localStorage
- [ ] Show a recent feeds list that lets users re-load a feed with one click
- [ ] Example: loading a valid feed adds it to the recent list and persists after refresh
- [ ] Negative case: failed/invalid URLs are not added to the recent list
